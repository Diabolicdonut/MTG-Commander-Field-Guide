The 99-card engineering problem

A Commander deck is not ninety-nine cards that match a theme. It is a mana system, card-flow system, interaction suite, resilience plan, and win condition that happen to express a theme.

The commander-dependence test

Goldfish after adding two commander taxes. Remove the best engine permanent. Exile the graveyard. If the deck stops functioning, replace redundant theme cards with independent draw, mana, or recursion.

Interaction is participation

Commander is multiplayer. A deck with no answers asks the other players to police every snowballing engine and combo. Interaction protects your own plan and improves the pod’s games.
