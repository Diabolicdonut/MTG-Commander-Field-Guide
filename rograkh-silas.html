3 · Game plan

How to sequence the deck

Mulligan for speed plus one protective layer. Rograkh enables mana and sacrifice effects; Silas supplies colors and occasional recursion.

Commander dependence test. Remove the commander twice in a practice game. A well-built list should still ramp, draw, interact, and advance its core theme.

6 · Bracket tuning

Move the same idea up or down responsibly

Bracket 5

Use current tournament lists, a defined metagame, efficient wins, free interaction, and aggressive mulligans.

Proxy policy

cEDH communities commonly separate gameplay from card ownership; agree on proxies before the event.

Testing

Goldfish is insufficient. Test into real pods, track mulligans, and review priority errors.
