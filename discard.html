3 · Game plan

How to sequence the deck

Use effects that hit all opponents and include payoff engines. Emptying hands without ending the game creates topdeck misery.

Commander dependence test. Remove the commander twice in a practice game. A well-built list should still ramp, draw, interact, and advance its core theme.

6 · Bracket tuning

Move the same idea up or down responsibly

Bracket 2

Remove Game Changers, fast mana, compact infinites, and efficient universal tutors. Favor visible board development.

Bracket 3

Allow up to three Game Changers, stronger engines, and one-turn finishes that require accumulated resources.

Bracket 4

Maximize speed and consistency without claiming to be a cEDH metagame deck. Use efficient tutors, free interaction, and compact wins.

7 · Wins and weaknesses

How games end—and how the deck is stopped

Common finishes

  • Waste Not storm turn
  • Discard damage
  • Tergrid theft engine

Structural weaknesses

  • Graveyard decks
  • Card-draw commanders
  • Drawing discard after hands are empty
8 · Table expectations

The multiplayer part of deck construction

Discard is a high-salt strategy. Tergrid, repeated hand locks, and mass discard deserve explicit pregame disclosure.
