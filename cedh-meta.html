What cEDH means

cEDH stands for competitive Elder Dragon Highlander. EDH—Elder Dragon Highlander—is Commander’s original community name. cEDH is not merely “very strong casual.” It is Commander played with metagame assumptions, optimized win lines, efficient interaction, tournament incentives, and victory as the shared objective.

Turbo
